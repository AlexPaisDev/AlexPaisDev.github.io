About

I'm a data and AI consultant at Noesis in Lisbon. My work is turning scattered sources into reporting people can trust: Python collectors that run themselves, pipelines on Azure and Microsoft Fabric, and Power BI that lives in one shared place instead of on someone's laptop.

I got here by an unusual route. I started in web development, spent almost two years as a data engineer at Fraudio building fraud-analytics dashboards on Databricks, then moved to Unity for naval simulation and game systems. That mix shows in how I work: clean architecture, an eye for performance, and the confidence to demo the result to the people who'll use it.

I completed my BSc in Computer Science & Engineering in 2026, including a semester at Macao Polytechnic Institute. Next, I'm looking for a data or AI role in Asia: remote, hybrid, or with relocation.
